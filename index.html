Frequently Asked Questions

What are your most popular schmears? +
Our Scallion and Veggie schmears are fan favorites, but don't sleep on our seasonal specials!
Do you offer gluten-free options? +
Currently, we focus on traditional wheat-based bagels to maintain our signature texture, but we offer salads and lox plates.
Where should I park? +
Fell St is busy! There is typically street parking on the surrounding blocks (Lyon or Baker), or limited short-term parking right out front.
Can I dine in? +
We are primarily a takeaway and delivery shop. We have limited outdoor seating, but we're right near the Panhandle for a park picnic!
